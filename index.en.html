Our Departments

An organization in transformation

From Clinical Operations to the Executive Office, every area of HealthCore is building the systems that will make future healthcare a reality.

🏥 Clinical Operations

Unifying two EHR systems (US & UK) into a common API. AI-assisted clinical documentation that reduces 35 min/day of administrative tasks per clinician.

🗓️ Patient Experience

Unified online booking platform for both markets. Smart reminders and no-show prediction to reduce the current 22% rate.

💰 Revenue Cycle

AI-assisted claims review to reduce the rejection rate from 14% to the industry standard. Unified US & UK dashboard.

🔒 Compliance & Governance

Centralized monitoring under HIPAA and UK GDPR. Consolidated audit trails and automated patient data request processing.

👥 People & Talent

Automated clinical onboarding, CME tracking with expiration alerts, and an HR chatbot for 200 employees across 12 locations in two countries.

💻 Technology & Data

Central API unifying patients, appointments, billing, and staff. Real-time telemetry from 12 locations and dashboards for the entire organization.
